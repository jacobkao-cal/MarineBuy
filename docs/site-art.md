# Site artwork (lifestyle photos)

These photos are decorative sailing artwork for account screens. They are **not** product photos, do not show the exact catalog models, and are not evidence of how any jacket performs. Brand logos and people visible in them do not imply endorsement of Marine Buy. Each placement is marked "Lifestyle photo, not a product image" and uses empty alt text because the image carries no information.

Before the site is public beyond a demo, confirm permission for each photo (or replace it) and record the source here.

Delivery: the 800px JPEGs are re-encoded with macOS `sips`. Where re-encoding made a file larger, the original is shipped unchanged (the 2100px WebP banner and the 480px portrait). Banners use `srcset` and `object-fit: cover` with `object-position` chosen so faces stay in frame.
